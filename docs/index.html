01 Care

Care that connects.

A radiologist opens a patient’s scan. Somewhere, a family is waiting for what it shows.

3verest · 21 regions worldwide, with the U.K. NHS among its users

Medical images, ready when the care team needs them

Medical images are large, heavily regulated and kept for decades, yet clinicians need them in seconds. Healthcare operators also need proof that data, backups and AI inference stay within their jurisdiction.

Equinix does not deliver the care. It provides the secure, local and directly connected foundation that lets 3verest keep critical medical data close, fast and governed.

Open the full story Faster AI development for earlier detection
Equinix contributes
In-country data center space as the physical anchor, carrier-neutral interconnection for private connections between care providers, medical device platforms and clouds, and Equinix Fabric Geo Zones that keep traffic inside defined geographic boundaries, even during failover.
Partners deliver
3verest designs and operates the sovereign healthcare clouds, including imaging archives and governed AI inference, offered through medical device partners such as Agfa, GE HealthCare and Philips.

Under 1 secondto retrieve a medical imageReported by 3verest for its own platform, compared with general public clouds. Specific to this customer example.

02 Discovery

Discovery that advances.

In Sydney, researchers compare one child’s cancer with cases studied on the other side of the world.

Children's Cancer Institute · Sydney, Australia, with collaborators in Philadelphia, Memphis and Heidelberg

Comparing notes across the world for every child with cancer

That precision medicine approach creates terabytes of data, and the most useful patterns appear only when it is compared with data from research centres in other countries. In-house computing could not scale to that, and the data needed protecting.

The discoveries belong to the researchers. Equinix helps by shortening the distance between their data and the people they need to compare it with.

Open the full story
Equinix contributes
Equinix Fabric gives the Institute a direct, private connection into its cloud providers and to collaborators worldwide, without the carrier costs usually involved in building that connectivity.
Partners deliver
The Institute’s researchers lead the science, from generating the data to integrating it with partner centres and pursuing new treatments.

03 Opportunity

Opportunity that grows.

A student walks into a data center for the first time and asks how it all works.

Equinix employees and the Equinix Foundation · Now scaling to every Equinix location

From a school tour to a technician’s badge

The work that keeps data centers running is largely hidden, so most young people never consider it as a career, or know how to start.

This is Equinix acting directly: our own people opening our own facilities, so students can picture themselves working there.

Open the full story An industry-wide answer to the technician shortage
Equinix contributes
Employee volunteers lead classroom talks, IBX tours and hands-on education days, and the programme offers a structured path into Equinix internships, apprenticeships and early-career roles. School partnerships began in Ashburn, Virginia, and Dallas, Texas.
Partners deliver
Schools and youth organisations, including Foundation partners such as i.c. stars, bring students and support them into training and work.

Nearly 2,000students reached in the two-year pilotTwo-year pilot in select communities across the Americas and Asia-Pacific, as reported in the 2025 Sustainability Report and Equinix blog.

04 Community

Community in connection.

In Minooka, Illinois, a public bike path runs along the data center site, and land is being donated for a future fire station.

Equinix with local governments, utilities and community leaders · Minooka, Illinois; San Jose, California; and U.S. communities where Equinix operates

A neighbour that plans to stay for decades

Data centers use power, water and land, and AI is accelerating demand. Communities rightly ask who pays for grid upgrades and what a new neighbour will give back.

Here the thread is not a cable. It is the long-term relationship between a data center and the town around it: roads, paths, power, taxes and local work.

Open the full story Bringing more people online, and helping them thrive thereColleagues giving time where they live
Equinix contributes
At the Minooka campus, Equinix is contributing $17M in public infrastructure, including roads, traffic signals and multi-use paths. Across the U.S., Equinix funds the power and water infrastructure its sites need, with long-term utility agreements such as a 10-year transmission agreement with ComEd and a collaboration with PG&E and the City of San Jose.
Partners deliver
Local leaders, utilities and residents set the priorities. Utilities plan and build the grid upgrades; local businesses supply the work.

$95M+with small and inclusive U.S. businessesU.S. inclusive procurement with certified and qualified businesses in calendar year 2025, as reported in Equinix’s U.S. Community Principles.

05 Responsible growth

Resources used thoughtfully.

Early swimmers in Saint-Denis are in water warmed, in part, by heat that once left a data center unused.

Equinix PA10 with the local heat network operator · Saint-Denis, Paris, France

Warm water for a neighbourhood pool in Saint-Denis

Servers produce heat that is usually released into the air. The neighbourhood around them needs heat for water and homes.

Here the thread is warm, not digital: heat leaving a data center and arriving as warm water in a public pool.

Open the full story Heat for homes, offices and two community pools in MarkhamRunning facilities more efficiently, year after year
Equinix contributes
PA10 opened in 2023 and was designed to recover and export heat from customer equipment. Heat is recovered at about 28°C, raised to 65°C by heat pumps and passed through a heat exchanger to the heat network operator.
Partners deliver
The heat network operator distributes the heat to the Olympic Aquatics Centre and the surrounding Plaine Saulnier neighbourhood.

1,000+homes expected to be warmedExpected reach of PA10 heat export, as stated by Equinix in April 2025. Specific to this site.

Role play: how to talk about Equinix community impact

Short practice scenarios, each tied to a story on this page. Practise them out loud with the coach: it plays the other person, then steps out and gives feedback against the five promises and the sourced figures.

  1. 1
    A customer asks what we give back.

    Open with 3verest or Children’s Cancer Institute: what the connection makes possible, then one promise. Two minutes.

  2. 2
    A neighbour worries about power and water.

    Lead with the Community Principles: ComEd, dry cooling, the Minooka bike path. Acknowledge, then specifics.

  3. 3
    A student asks what a data center job looks like.

    Kayla’s path and Pathways to Tech, in your own words. One minute.

  4. 4
    A journalist asks for a number.

    Use one figure with its scope and period, and name the source. Practise saying the caveat naturally.

The coach is a conversational voice agent (ElevenLabs) that knows only this page's stories, promises and sourced figures. Choose who it plays, talk or type, then ask for feedback. Conversations are recorded by ElevenLabs for review. Yoodli exercises can be linked alongside later.

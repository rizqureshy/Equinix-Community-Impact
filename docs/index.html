  1. 01Public infrastructure investmentInvest in roads, paths and public services around our sites, and fund the power and water infrastructure our sites need.KeptA public bike path and land for a future fire station in Minooka, Illinois.
  2. 02Power responsibilityPay our full share of grid costs so our demand does not raise residents’ bills.KeptA 10-year transmission agreement with ComEd that the utility described as a model.
  3. 03Jobs and skills developmentCreate quality jobs and educational pathways for tomorrow’s workforce.KeptSchool partnerships through Pathways to Tech, starting in Ashburn and Dallas.
  4. 04Natural resource protectionUse water and land carefully and report transparently.KeptAvoiding water-based cooling at new sites in water-stressed areas.

A hairline line drawing in one single red ink on pure white paper, in the manner of an architect's first site sketch made before anything is built: one confident continuous pen weight throughout, open linework, no shading, no hatching, no grey tones, no text, no lettering, no numbers, no logos. Beneath the lines, one flat pale wash of warm sand colour laid loosely under the path and the building only, like a single marker stroke that does not stay inside the lines; nothing else is coloured. A few short dimension ticks along one edge of the path and one small registration cross in a corner, as on a plan. Generous white space. Portrait format.

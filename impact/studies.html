Three opening studies

Each study uses the same warm, restrained language and the same live 3D community. They differ in what a visitor meets first. Open any study at full size to try it. The chosen direction is A, refined with the human images from B.

Chosen direction

A. The living model, with human windows

The community model fills the stage like an architectural model on a table, with the headline in the calm space above it. Three small human images sit beside the headline and lead straight into their chapters. People come first; the infrastructure is there to discover.
